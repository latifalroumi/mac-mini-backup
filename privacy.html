Privacy policy

Mac mini backup is used solely by its owner to back up the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Data accessed: only the backup files the app itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (Google permission drive.file).
  • Data use: the files are stored so the owner can restore his computer. Nothing else is done with them.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. Nothing is sent anywhere except the owner's Google Drive.
  • Retention: backups older than 14 days are deleted automatically.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
