Mac mini backup

Mac mini backup is a private, single-user tool. Every night it backs up its owner's own Mac (code, settings and application data) to the owner's own Google Drive, and keeps 14 days of earlier versions.

Google access: it uses only the Google Drive permission drive.file, which lets it see and manage only the backup files it creates itself. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.
